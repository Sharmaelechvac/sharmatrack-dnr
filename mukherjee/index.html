Electrical scope of work

  1. 400A service with Siemens MC0816B1400RLTM meter combo (two 200A mains).
  2. Two Siemens PN4040L1200 200A 40-space plug-on-neutral panels, fed with 4/0 AL SER cable.
  3. 23 dual-function breakers.
  4. 48 Amico 4-inch canless wafer lights.
  5. 79 tamper-resistant receptacles, plus 2 garage-opener receptacles and 2 floor receptacles.
  6. 11 exterior weather-resistant receptacles.
  7. 50 switches, including 19 three-way switches.
  8. 6 smoke alarms and 4 smoke/CO alarms, interconnected.
  9. 12 fan-rated boxes.
  10. 6 A/C disconnects.
  11. Dedicated 30A elevator circuit to a Siemens GF221N safety switch.
  12. Grounding electrodes and bonding.
